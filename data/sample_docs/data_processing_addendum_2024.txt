1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") governs the processing of Personal Data under European Union data protection laws (GDPR).

4. DATA BREACH MANAGEMENT
4.1 Breach Notification Timeline: Notwithstanding any general corporate policies or standard security response timelines (such as 72-hour general policy windows), data breach notifications involving EU citizen Personal Data must be formally communicated to Client's Data Protection Officer within twenty-four (24) hours of initial discovery.

7. DATA RETENTION & ERASURE
7.1 Specialized Retention Schedule: Inactive account logs containing EU citizen personal data must be permanently erased within ninety (90) days of account termination, overriding standard 12-month log retention schedules.
