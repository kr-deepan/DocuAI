================================================================================
CORPORATE DATA SECURITY & INCIDENT RESPONSE POLICY
================================================================================
Document ID: POL-SEC-2023-V1
Effective Date: March 10, 2023
Applicability: All Operations & Enterprise Engagements

1. OBJECTIVE
This policy sets forth standard operational requirements for data security, encryption, and incident handling across all enterprise software offerings.

3. INCIDENT NOTIFICATION & RESPONSE
3.1 Detection: The Security Operations Center (SOC) monitors infrastructure 24/7/365.
3.2 Incident Notification Window: In the event of a confirmed security incident or data breach affecting enterprise systems, the Provider shall notify affected clients within seventy-two (72) hours of breach verification.

5. DATA RETENTION AND DISPOSAL
5.1 System Logs: Inactive account system logs and user access records shall be retained in secure archive storage for twelve (12) months prior to automated permanent deletion.
5.2 Media Sanitization: Hard drives and storage volumes are purged in accordance with NIST SP 800-88 guidelines upon decommissioning.
