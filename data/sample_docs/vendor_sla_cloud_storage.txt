================================================================================
CLOUD STORAGE SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (VENDOR POLICY)
================================================================================
Document ID: POL-SLA-STORAGE-2024
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

1. SERVICE OVERVIEW
This SLA defines maintenance windows, performance metrics, and support escalation protocols for underlying Object Storage services.

2. MAINTENANCE WINDOWS & DOWNTIME
2.1 Scheduled Maintenance: Scheduled routine maintenance occurs every Sunday between 02:00 UTC and 04:00 UTC. Scheduled downtime during this window does not count against monthly uptime SLA calculations.
2.2 Emergency Maintenance: Provider reserves the right to execute emergency security patches with 2 hours advance notice.
